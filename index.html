// 01 — About

Builder, not just a learner.

I am Richard Gladson, a person who enjoys building beyond what already exists: taking software apart, refining it, making it faster, smarter, and more capable, then connecting different tools and technologies to create something entirely new. I'm driven by ideas that begin as rough concepts and become working products through experimentation, iteration, and problem-solving. I like finding possibilities others overlook, building the missing pieces myself, and continuously improving every part of a system.

Once I start building, I don't stop at "works." I keep testing, refining, debugging, and polishing until the product feels complete, reliable, powerful, and as close to perfect as I can make it.
